Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook (2026)

Working hours
The office is open from 9:00 to 18:00, Sunday to Thursday. Friday and Saturday are days off.
Employees may work remotely two days per week with approval from their team lead.

Leave
Every employee gets 21 days of paid annual leave and 10 days of paid sick leave.
Annual leave must be requested at least one week in advance.

Expenses
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 40 USD per day.
Taxi rides inside the city are reimbursed up to 25 USD per trip and need a receipt.
Expense reports are submitted in the finance portal before the 5th of the next month.

Equipment
New employees receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset on their first day.
Replacement laptops are ordered by IT after the employee opens a ticket.

Security
The company VPN must be used when connecting from outside the office.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 90 days.
Lost laptops must be reported to security@northwind.example within one hour.

Support
IT support answers tickets from 8:00 to 20:00. The internal support number is 1400.
Payroll questions go to payroll@northwind.example.
